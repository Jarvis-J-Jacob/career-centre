# Community code of conduct

Career Centre welcomes people with different career histories, locations,
abilities and technical backgrounds.

Participants must be respectful, specific and considerate. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, deliberate humiliation and the
publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

This project handles unusually sensitive career material. Never publish
somebody else's CV, contact details, identity information, work-right evidence
or confidential employer material without explicit permission. Prefer the
repository's synthetic fixtures.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove contributions that break these rules and
may restrict participation when necessary. Use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for a
sensitive security or privacy report.
